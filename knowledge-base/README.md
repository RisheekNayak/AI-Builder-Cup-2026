# ShipSure Knowledge Base Sources

This document contains all the regulatory references and URLs for the manual download of compliance documents used to populate this knowledge base.

## DGFT SCOMET Appendix 3
- **Source**: Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT)
- **URL**: [https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=scomet](https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=scomet)
- **Document**: Appendix 3 to Schedule 2 of ITC (HS) Classifications of Export and Import Items.
- **Notes**: Manually verify Category 0 to 8 lists. Regular updates are issued via Notifications. 

## ITC(HS) Schedule
- **Source**: Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT)
- **URL**: [https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=itchs-import-export](https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=itchs-import-export)
- **Document**: ITC (HS) 2022 - Schedule 2 (Export Policy).
- **Notes**: Contains 8-digit HS codes. Ensure to check latest amendments.

## India-UAE CEPA Text
- **Source**: Department of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce and Industry
- **URL**: [https://commerce.gov.in/international-trade/trade-agreements/comprehensive-economic-partnership-agreement-between-the-government-of-the-republic-of-india-and-the-government-of-the-united-arab-emirates-uae/](https://commerce.gov.in/international-trade/trade-agreements/comprehensive-economic-partnership-agreement-between-the-government-of-the-republic-of-india-and-the-government-of-the-united-arab-emirates-uae/)
- **Document**: Full text of the CEPA agreement, including Annexures on Rules of Origin and Tariff Reduction Schedules.
- **Notes**: Effective from May 1, 2022.

## ICEGATE Format Specs
- **Source**: ICEGATE (Indian Customs Electronic Data Interchange Gateway)
- **URL**: [https://www.icegate.gov.in/guidelines/message-guidelines](https://www.icegate.gov.in/guidelines/message-guidelines)
- **Document**: RES (Remote EDI System) Message Implementation Guidelines for Shipping Bill (SB).
- **Notes**: Check for versions matching the current ICEGATE system updates (e.g., V 1.5/2.0).

## FTP 2023 Handbook
- **Source**: Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT)
- **URL**: [https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=ft-policy](https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=ft-policy)
- **Document**: Foreign Trade Policy 2023 and Handbook of Procedures (HBP) 2023.
- **Notes**: Reference for RoDTEP, Drawback claims, and general export guidelines.

*Information accurate as of September 2026. Verify periodically for new notifications.*
